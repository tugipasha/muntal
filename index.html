About — Chapter I

Diplomacy is a discipline.

MUNTAL was founded on a single conviction: that debate, done properly, sharpens the mind more than any classroom exercise. We bring together delegates who arrive having read the background guide twice, not once.

Held annually in Izmir, the conference gathers students from across the region for a week of committee sessions, crisis simulations and resolutions that are actually meant to be defensible.
