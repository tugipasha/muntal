About — Chapter I

Diplomacy is a discipline.

MUNTAL was founded on a single conviction: that debate, done properly, sharpens the mind more than any classroom exercise. We bring together delegates who arrive having read the background guide twice, not once.

Held annually in Izmir, the conference gathers students from across the region for a week of committee sessions, crisis simulations and resolutions that are actually meant to be defensible.

Committees — Chapter II

Four councils, one week.

  • 01 Security Council High-stakes crisis committee with a live update mechanism.
  • 02 General Assembly Broad policy debate on global development and rights.
  • 03 Historical Crisis A single-delegation committee reworking a turning point in history.
  • 04 Press Corps Delegates report, photograph and publish the conference as it unfolds.

Apply — Chapter IV

How to join us.

  • I Choose a committee Review the guides and rank your top three preferences.
  • II Submit your application A short form and a position statement on your assigned topic.
  • III Receive your allocation Delegations are matched by committee, experience and preference.
